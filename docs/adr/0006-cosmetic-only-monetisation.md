# Monetisation is cosmetic-only; every gameplay advantage is earnable free

Nothing sold for real money may affect who wins. Paid ranks grant cosmetic and social perks —
prefixes, chat colour, particles, pets, join messages, nicknames, Discord status. **Every** perk
that touches gameplay — sell multipliers, kits with gear, crate keys whose tables contain
gameplay items, `/fly`, extra homes that shorten travel, claim bonuses, keep-inventory — is earned
in game, on a free progression ladder open to everyone.

This is Mojang's rule, not a preference. The Usage Guidelines forbid selling anything "designed to
give someone a competitive gameplay advantage or make another player's experience worse", and the
practical guidance names the exact things factions servers habitually sell as violations: `/fly`,
enchanted kits, money and drop multipliers, and **paid crate keys containing gameplay-relevant
items**. The genre matters — on skyblock a paid tier can read as "the intended pace", but on
factions and PvP "monetised advantages change who wins". Enforcement is a server blacklist, which
would end the project.

**Considered:** the common factions-server model (donor ranks granting `/fly`, kits, sell
multipliers and gameplay crate keys) — rejected as a rule violation regardless of how widespread it
is; a middle path where only *small* advantages are sold — rejected because the line is
unenforceable in review and the downside is terminal; cosmetics plus "priority queue" — parked, see
consequences.

**Consequences:** the free ladder is the real progression system and must be designed first and
best; the paid ladder hangs off cosmetics only and must still feel worth buying, which means
cosmetics need genuine effort spent on them. Spec §12's "premium currency should primarily provide
cosmetics/perks and should not be required for competitive progression **unless explicitly
configured**" is tightened here: there is no configuration under which gems buy competitive
progression. Crates split by how the key is obtained, not by rarity alone — an earned key may
contain anything, a purchased key may contain only cosmetics. Priority queue on a full server is
arguably access rather than advantage; it stays undecided until we ever have a queue.

## The two ladders

Named on different axes so a player can tell instantly which is which: **free ranks are life
stages, paid ranks are raptor species.**

| | Free — earned | Paid — cosmetic |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hatchling | Kestrel |
| 2 | Fledgling | Osprey |
| 3 | Flier | Falcon |
| 4 | Soarer | Hawk |
| 5 | Skyborne | Raven |

Free ranks are earned by playtime, quests and in-game achievement, and carry the gameplay perks:
sell-multiplier steps, kit access, home slots, claim bonuses. Paid ranks carry prefix, colour,
particles, pets, join message, nickname and Discord role — and stack with any free rank, because
they are orthogonal.

**Earned keys** (Feather, Plume, Talon, Pinion, Raven) come from voting, bosses, KOTH and the Aerie,
and may contain gameplay rewards. **Store keys** are a separate cosmetic-only line. Voting is the
interesting case: it earns the server something real, and the reward is free to the player, so a
vote key with gameplay contents is fine.
